Further school hours

Mondays, Tuesdays and Thursdays in October and November are open for schools, libraries and groups across Oxfordshire.

A school hour is one lesson long:

  • Length One hour
  • Speakers Two to four veterans
  • Stories Short first-person accounts
  • Then Questions and answers with the room

No parade and no politics lesson. People who have served, a story each, and then the room gets to ask.
