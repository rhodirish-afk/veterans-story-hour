Around Oxfordshire

Pilot outreach is active across the county — schools, parish councils, Combined Cadet Force contacts, and veterans' networks. Ducklington Parish Council has circulated the invitation. Interest has also come through school CCF channels, including at St Edward's Oxford.

What comes next

After the pilot, the plan is further hours in schools, libraries and youth centres across Oxfordshire, and then further afield. If you would like to host a session, or know a veteran who might speak, please get in touch.
