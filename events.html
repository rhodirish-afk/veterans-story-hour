Schools in Oxfordshire

The first school visits in Oxfordshire are being booked now. We will publish the schools and dates here as each booking is confirmed.

A school hour is one lesson long:

  • Length One hour
  • Speakers Two veterans
  • Stories Fifteen minutes each
  • Then Thirty minutes of questions and answers with the children

No parade and no politics lesson. Two people who have served, a story each, and then the room gets to ask.

Schools that want a slot should write now — the diary is filling.

Around Oxfordshire

Outreach is active across the county — schools, parish councils, Combined Cadet Force contacts, and veterans' networks. Ducklington Parish Council has circulated the invitation. Interest has also come through school CCF channels, including at St Edward's Oxford.

What comes next

After the pilot and the first school hours, the plan is further sessions in libraries and youth centres across Oxfordshire, and then further afield.
