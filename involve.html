Speak · Host · Support

Get involved

If you are a veteran willing to speak, a school, library or youth centre that would host a session, or simply want to support the work, get in touch.

Veterans

Speakers are welcome. We ask for a short first-person account of service — ten or fifteen minutes. It is not a parade and it is not a politics lesson. Logistics and support for speakers are being sorted with local veterans' networks — get in touch for details.

Hosts

Schools, libraries and youth centres across Oxfordshire — and beyond — that would welcome a visit later in the year.

Supporters

Anyone who wants this work to take root — parish councils, veterans' groups, cadet forces, and neighbours. Supported by a Civic Future Action Grant. Civic Future are the partners who have helped set up Veterans' Story Hour. Starting in Oxfordshire with the aim of going national.
