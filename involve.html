Get involved

If you are a veteran willing to speak, a school, library or youth centre that would host a session, or simply want to support the work, get in touch.

Veterans

We need veterans willing to give a short first-person account of service — ten or fifteen minutes. It is not a parade and it is not a politics lesson. For the pilot and later hours we can pay £50 to cover time and a little of the expenses.

Hosts

Schools, libraries and youth centres across Oxfordshire — and beyond — that would welcome a visit later in the year.

Supporters

Anyone who wants this work to take root. Supported by a Civic Future Action Grant. Starting in Oxfordshire with the aim of going national.
