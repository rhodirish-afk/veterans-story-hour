Your request has reached Veterans' Story Hour. Thank you for wanting to hear these stories.

What happens next

  1. Rory reads every request personally and will reply by email or phone to talk through what you have in mind.
  2. We will suggest a date and speakers that fit your group. Nothing is fixed until we have agreed it together.
  3. Once agreed, you will get a short confirmation with the arrangements, and a reminder the week before.

If you asked about a pub evening in the winter Yarning series, there is nothing for you to arrange on hosting: Rory hosts every evening himself, and any welcoming pub or space works.

You should also receive an automatic email confirming your request arrived. If it does not appear, please check your junk folder.
