Book a Story Hour

Veterans of the British Armed Forces giving short, first-person accounts of service, followed by your questions. Tell us a little about your group and the dates that suit, and we will come back to you.

How it works

  • Schools: an assembly, classroom or sixth-form session of 45 to 60 minutes, with one to three speakers. Free to schools, supported by a Civic Future Action Grant.
  • Pubs and community groups: an evening of stories with time for questions, part of the winter Yarning series (summer sessions in beer gardens and other spaces to follow). Rory hosts every evening himself, so you don't need to provide a host, and any welcoming pub or space works. Tell us what you have in mind.
  • Sending this form is a request, not a booking. We will reply to talk through dates, speakers and any safeguarding arrangements before anything is confirmed.
