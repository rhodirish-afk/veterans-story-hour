Please complete this form and return it to the school before the visit. One form per child.

1. The visit

Veterans’ Story Hour brings Armed Forces veterans into the classroom to give a short, first-person account of service. It is not a parade and it is not a politics lesson.

  • Length: one hour (one lesson).
  • Speakers: two veterans.
  • Stories: fifteen minutes each.
  • Then: thirty minutes of questions and answers with the children.

School staff remain in the room throughout. The school stays in charge of the class. Speakers are asked to keep language and detail suitable for the year group. A child who does not take part will be given an alternative activity by the school.
