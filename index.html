Lived service · Civic pride

Veterans' Story Hour

Armed Forces veterans into schools, youth centres and libraries — to recount lived stories of bravery, honour and quiet pride.

You will no doubt have heard of Drag Queen Story Hour, where colourful drag artists visit schools and libraries to tell stories to young and older children. We want to harness some of that energy and design to bring Armed Forces veterans instead — men and women who have borne the weight of service — into classrooms, youth centres and libraries.

The narratives we propose are those of patriotism and civic pride — stories drawn from every background, every race, yet all of them British and all of them unashamed of that identity. We require fresh channels through which these living testimonies may reach the young — channels that speak with the authority of experience rather than the rhetoric of ideology.

Inaugural pilot — Monday 14 September 2026

09:00–11:00 · The Vine Inn, 11 Abingdon Road, Cumnor, Oxfordshire OX2 9QN · Map

British Armed Forces veterans share short first-person accounts of service, resilience and comradeship. Tea, coffee, homemade cakes and bacon rolls. Places are limited.

Who this is for

Veterans willing to speak. Schools, libraries and youth centres willing to host. Supporters who want the next generation to hear what service actually means — from those who practised it.

Supported by a Civic Future Action Grant. Starting in Oxfordshire, with the aim of going national.
